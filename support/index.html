Subscriptions and purchases

Restore a purchase

Open Closer, go to Settings, choose Closer Plus, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure your device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions and select Closer. Deleting the app does not automatically cancel a subscription.

Premium access is not appearing

Confirm you have an active Apple subscription, reopen Closer, and use Restore Purchases. If access still does not appear, email support with the product name and approximate purchase date. Do not send full receipts containing sensitive information unless requested.

Account and data

Delete your account

In Closer, open Settings, find the Account section, and choose Delete Account. This removes the account and associated synchronized content from Closer’s active systems. You can also email us for help with a privacy request.

Photos and notifications

Photo and notification permissions can be changed in the iPhone Settings app under Closer. Closer only accesses photos you select through Apple’s photo picker.
